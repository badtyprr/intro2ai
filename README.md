A six-week plan for learning to use AI tools at your own pace.

You do not need any technical background. You do not need to like AI. You are allowed to finish this plan and decide AI is not for you. The goal is that whatever you decide, you decide it from understanding, not from guessing.

## How this plan works

- **About 1 to 2 hours per week.** Split it however you like.
- **Watch and read first, try things later.** Weeks 1 and 2 involve no AI tools at all. You will not touch a chatbot until Week 3.
- **Your concerns are part of the curriculum.** Many worries about AI are reasonable. Week 2 and the [Concerns and Safety](concerns-and-safety.md) page take them seriously instead of brushing them aside.
- **Every week ends with a check-in.** If you want to pause, repeat a week, or stop, that is a valid outcome.
- **Every week has a free option.** A free San Jose Public Library card unlocks the courses in Weeks 4 and 5. One paid alternative is mentioned but never required.

## Study Buddy (optional AI tutor)

If you would like company while you learn, Study Buddy is an AI chatbot set up to walk through this plan with you, patiently and at your pace. It will remind you of the safety rules, admit when it might be wrong, and respect it if you want to slow down or stop. Using it is entirely optional; the plan works fine on paper.

## Ground rules

1. You set the pace. There is no deadline.
2. You can skip anything that feels pointless or uncomfortable.
3. Never type anything into an AI tool that you would not write on a postcard. (More on this in Week 2.)
4. If something confuses you, that is the course's fault, not yours.
5. Asking for help is fine. So is doing it alone.
