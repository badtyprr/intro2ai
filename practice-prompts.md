# Practice Prompts

Low-stakes things to try. Copy any of these into a chatbot, or change them to fit your life. Remember the postcard rule: nothing private.

## Getting comfortable

- Explain what you are and what you can and cannot do, in simple words.
- What is a good question to ask you if I have never used a chatbot before?
- Tell me a short, cheerful fact about cats.

## Everyday

- Give me three ideas for a quick weeknight dinner using what I have: [list a few ingredients].
- Write a short, polite message to [a teacher / a neighbor / a coworker] asking to [your request].
- I need to pack for a [number]-day trip to [place] in [month]. Make me a simple packing list.
- Explain this in plain English: [paste a confusing paragraph from a letter or instructions, with any personal details removed].
- I play [a sport or activity] about [how often]. Suggest a simple way to track my progress over a month.
- Help me plan a simple birthday party for a [age]-year-old with about [number] guests.

## Checking its work

- What is the population of [a town you know]? (Then check it yourself.)
- Tell me about [a hobby or topic you know well]. (Then look for mistakes.)
- Give me a famous quote about kindness, and tell me who said it. (Then search to see if it is real.)

## Asking it to try again

- That was too long. Make it shorter.
- Make that sound friendlier.
- That is not right. Actually, [correct information]. Please fix it.
- Explain that again, more simply.

## Learning something new

- I want to learn [a skill]. What are three small first steps?
- Quiz me with five easy questions about [a topic I am learning].
- What is the difference between [two things you have wondered about]?
