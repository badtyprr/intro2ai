# Week 1: What AI Is (and Is Not)

**Time:** about 1 hour
**Uses AI tools:** No

## Goal

Understand, in plain words, what tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude actually do, and what they cannot do.

## Before you start (5 minutes)

On a piece of paper or in a note on your phone, write down:

1. What worries you about AI? List as many things as come to mind. Be specific if you can.
2. Is there anything about it you are curious about, even a little?

Keep this list. You will come back to it in Week 2 and Week 6.

## The one idea to take away

AI chatbots work by predicting what words are likely to come next, based on enormous amounts of text they were trained on. That is why they:

- sound fluent and confident, even when they are wrong
- can be very helpful for writing, summarizing, and explaining
- do not "know" things the way a person does, and do not have intentions or feelings toward you

A useful mental picture: an extremely well-read assistant who sometimes makes things up without realizing it. Helpful, but you check its work.
