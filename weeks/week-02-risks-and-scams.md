# Week 2: Real Risks, Scams, and Deepfakes

**Time:** about 1.5 hours
**Uses AI tools:** No

## Goal

Learn which concerns about AI are well founded, how to protect yourself, and how to spot AI-made scams and fake images. This week matters whether or not you ever use AI yourself, because AI-powered scams reach everyone.

## Part 1: Lesson (about 45 minutes)

Go through the San Jose Public Library's free **AI 102** lesson. No library card is needed.

- Link: https://www.sjpl.org/blogs/post/learning-digital-skills-artificial-intelligence/ (look for "AI 102" under Digital Skill Building Lessons)

It goes deeper into how AI is trained, how to recognize fake faces and deepfakes, and how to use AI safely.

## Part 2: Read the Concerns and Safety page (about 20 minutes)

Read [Concerns and Safety](../concerns-and-safety.md). Then take out your list of worries from Week 1 and compare.

- Which of your worries are covered there?
- Which ones are not? Those are worth asking about in a workshop or with someone you trust.

## Part 3 (optional): Attend a free in-person workshop

The San Jose Public Library runs free hands-on classes on AI basics, scams, and deepfakes at branch libraries. The AI Center for Civic and Social Good at the Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Library downtown also offers free AI classes for beginners.

- Library events: https://www.sjpl.org/events (search "AI")
- Some people find a live instructor and a room of other beginners much more comfortable than learning alone. Others prefer privacy. Either is fine.

## The one rule to take away

**The postcard rule:** never type anything into an AI tool that you would not be comfortable writing on a postcard. That means no passwords, account numbers, ID numbers, medical details, or private information about other people.

## Check-in (5 minutes)

- Do you feel better or worse informed about the risks than last week?
- Are you ready to try a short, guided conversation with a chatbot next week? If not, it is fine to repeat this week, attend a workshop first, or stop here.
