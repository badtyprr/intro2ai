# Week 3: Your First Conversation with a Chatbot

**Time:** about 1 hour
**Uses AI tools:** Yes, briefly

## Goal

Have a short, low-stakes conversation with an AI chatbot and see for yourself what it is like.

## Before you start

- Choose a chatbot. Common free options are ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Gemini (gemini.google.com), and Claude (claude.ai). They are similar enough that the choice does not matter much for this week.
- You may need to create a free account with an email address. Use the postcard rule from Week 2 for anything you type.
- Decide whether you want someone sitting with you. Having a person nearby for the first try can help. So can doing it completely alone with no one watching.

## Step 1: Look at the privacy settings (10 minutes)

Before you type anything, look in the settings for:

- an option about whether your conversations are used to train the AI (you can usually turn this off)
- how to delete a conversation
- how to delete your account

You do not have to change anything. The point is knowing you are in control and where the controls are.

## Step 2: Three harmless questions (20 minutes)

Try these, one at a time. Read each answer slowly.

1. "Explain what a chatbot is, as if I have never used one."
2. "Give me three ideas for a simple dinner using chicken, rice, and broccoli."
3. Ask it about something you already know well, like a hobby, a place you have lived, or a skill you have. See whether it gets things right.

Question 3 is the most important one. When you already know the answer, you can see for yourself when AI is helpful and when it is confidently wrong.

## Step 3: Try correcting it (10 minutes)

If anything in its answers was wrong or unhelpful, tell it so in plain words, for example: "That is not right. Actually..." Notice how it responds.

## Step 4: Close it (5 minutes)

Delete the conversation if you want to. You are done for the week.

## Check-in (5 minutes)

- How did it feel? Uncomfortable, boring, interesting, surprising? All of these are normal.
- Did it get anything wrong in Question 3?
- Do you want to keep going, repeat this week, or stop?
