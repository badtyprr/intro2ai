# Week 4: Everyday Uses

**Time:** about 2 hours (can be split over several days)
**Uses AI tools:** Yes

## Goal

Learn a few practical, everyday ways AI can save time, and how to ask it clearly for what you want.

## Part 1: Course (about 1.5 hours)

**Free option (recommended): LinkedIn Learning through the library.** A free San Jose Public Library card gives you LinkedIn Learning, which has over 120 courses on AI.

- Get a library card (free, online or at any branch): https://www.sjpl.org
- Then open LinkedIn Learning from the library's AI page: https://www.sjpl.org/blogs/post/learning-digital-skills-artificial-intelligence/
- Search for "generative AI for beginners" or "ChatGPT for beginners." Pick a course that is marked Beginner, was updated recently, and is under 2 hours. If the first one you try feels too technical or too work-focused, stop and pick another. That is not failing.

**Paid option: Google AI Essentials on Coursera.** Taught by people at Google, no technical background needed. It is not free: it costs $49 per month after a 7-day free trial, and Coursera offers financial aid. If you choose it, do Module 1 (Introduction to AI) and Module 2 (Maximize Productivity with AI Tools) this week.

- Link: https://www.coursera.org/learn/google-ai-essentials

Either course is aimed partly at people using AI at work. If an example is about the workplace, just think of a similar task from your own life.

## Part 2: Practice (about 30 minutes)

Pick two or three items from the "Everyday" section of [Practice Prompts](../practice-prompts.md) and try them.

## The one skill to take away

**Be specific.** AI gives better answers when you tell it:

- what you want ("a short email")
- who it is for ("to my child's teacher")
- the tone ("polite and brief")
- anything it should include or avoid

Compare "Write an email" with "Write a short, polite email to my son's teacher asking to move our meeting from Tuesday to Thursday." The second one gets a much more useful answer.

## Check-in (5 minutes)

- Did any task actually save you time or effort?
- Was there anything you expected it to do well that it did badly?
