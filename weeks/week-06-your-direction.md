# Week 6: Choosing Your Own Next Step

**Time:** your choice
**Uses AI tools:** your choice

## Goal

Decide, based on what you have learned, what (if anything) you want to do next.

## Step 1: Look back (15 minutes)

Take out the list of worries you wrote in Week 1. For each one:

- Is it still a worry?
- Has it changed? For example, "AI is scary" might have become "I do not trust AI with personal information," which is more specific and easier to act on.
- Is it something you now know how to protect yourself against?

## Step 2: Pick a direction

Any of these is a good outcome.

**A. Stop here.** You understand what AI is, its real risks, and how to spot AI scams. That is more than many people know. You can come back any time.

**B. Use it lightly.** Keep a chatbot for a few specific tasks you found useful, like drafting emails or meal ideas, and nothing else.

**C. Keep taking courses.** Continue with more beginner courses on LinkedIn Learning through the library (free), or take Google AI Essentials on Coursera ($49 per month after a 7-day free trial; financial aid available).
- https://www.coursera.org/learn/google-ai-essentials

**D. Understand how it works more deeply.** Elements of AI is a free course focused on how AI works and what it means for society, rather than on using tools. Good if understanding matters more to you than using.
- https://www.elementsofai.com

**E. Learn with other people.** Attend a free class at a San Jose Public Library branch or at the AI Center at the Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Library downtown.
- https://www.sjpl.org/events

## Step 3: Write it down (5 minutes)

Write one or two sentences about what you decided and why. If you chose to continue, note which week you will start and when.
